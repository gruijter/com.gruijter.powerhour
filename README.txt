Power by the Hour turns Homey into your own Energy Management System (EMS): lowest energy costs and most self-consumption, set up from your phone.
It plans your home battery, charges your car at the cheapest time or on solar, and starts your washing machine at the cheapest moment. No battery? Everything else works too.
Self-learning: home load, car trips, appliance programs, and a solar forecast without orientation or panel data.
Any brand: P1 meters, inverters, batteries, EV chargers, smart plugs. Any contract: dynamic (15 min or hourly) or fixed, with or without netting, capacity tariff.
See the use and costs of electricity, gas and water per hour, day, month and year, and what your always-on devices cost.
